This makes 4 servings, but includes portions if you want to make a bunch of the sauce ahead of time.

# Equipment  

- 1 large frying pan or wok  
- 1 medium pot  
- 1 cooking sheet  
- Knife  
- Cutting board  
- Measuring spoons and cups  
- Bowl or mason jar for sauce  

# Ingredients

## Sauce 

### 4 servings  

- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce  
- 6 tbsp oyster sauce  
- 4 tbsp light soy sauce  
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar  
- 2 tbsp sugar  
  
### 20 servings

- 1 cup dark soy sauce  
- 2 cups oyster sauce  
- 1.25 cups light soy sauce  
- 1 cup white vinegar  
- 1 cup sugar  
  
# Other Ingredients (4 servings)
- Vegetable oil  
- 4 cloves garlic  
- 1 extra firm tofu package  
- 4 large eggs  
- 4 broccoli heads (Chinese and/or North American)  
- 2 carrots   
- 1/4 to 1/2 package dried brown rice noodles  
- 1-2 tbsp corn starch  
- 1/2 large onion  
- (Any other vegetables, fruit, or mushrooms you want to add)  



# Instructions

The Tofu and Noodles steps require some time, and you may want to get the oven running and water boiling while you are prepping the sauces and other ingredients.

## Prepare the tofu

1. Heat oven to 425F  
1. Line cooking sheet with a little bit of vegetable oil  
1. Place cooking sheet into oven while it heats  
1. Cut tofu block into small, equally sized pieces  
1. Coat the tofu in corn starch  
1. After the oven is at 425F, place the tofu on the cooking sheet and into the oven for 15 minutes  

## Sauce prep  

Mix all the sauce ingredients into a bowl or mason jar  

## Other ingredient prep  

1. Cut garlic into small pieces  
1.  Trim ends of broccoli and separate leaves from stems.  
1. Cut carrots into thin slices  
1. Break eggs and beat until unified yellow liquid  
1. Slice onion into small slices  
1. (Cut any other vegetables, fruit, or mushrooms you want to add) 

## Prep for noodles  

1. Fill the pot 3/4 full with water  
1. Boil the water  


## Check the tofu 

1. After 15 minutes, rotate the tofu pieces  
1. Put the tofu back into the oven for 10 minutes  


## Star cooking noodles

You may want to do these steps at the same time as pan frying in the next section.

1. Add the noodles until tender  
1. Drain the water from the noodles  

## Pan fry things!

1. Heat frying pan  
1. After warm, add oil and let it sit for a minute  
1. Fry the garlic and onions until golden, then move to a large bowl  
1. Fry the carrots until tender, then move to the large bowl with other cooked veggies  
1. Fry the stems of the broccoli until tender, then move to the large bowl with other cooked veggies  
1. Fry the leafy ends of the broccoli until tender, then move to the large bowl with other cooked veggies  
1. Fry the egg until scrambled  
1. Turn off the heat  
1. Add the vegetables, noodles, and sauce to the frying pan with egg  
1. Stir until mixed
